Medkart Savings Journey — MVP test strategy, defect workflow and pilot release controls

Status: Draft for prototype validation and implementation feasibility review Related artifacts: PRD v0.1 • Feature Specification v0.1 • QA Tracker workbook MVP scope: Linked purchase history, digital invoice, explainable savings and correction handling Execution state: 40 test cases designed; execution status is Not Run Evidence state: Eight sample defects demonstrate reporting structure; none are production claims Version: 0.1 • 19 July 2026 Disclosure: Independent candidate exercise; no access to Medkart production systems

Quality objective: Prove that store-purchase records are linked, explained and corrected without exposing another customer’s data, misstating savings or interrupting store checkout.

Evidence rule: A designed test is not a passed test. A sample bug is not an observed defect. Execution status changes only after authorized testing with reproducible evidence.

1. Scope and quality risks

1.1 In scope

  • Transaction ingestion, eligibility, idempotency and delayed-event recovery.
  • Unique account matching, fail-closed ambiguous/missing matches and authorization.
  • Purchase history, detail, digital invoice and transaction lifecycle states.
  • Approved savings calculation, explanation, unavailable states and rounding.
  • Issue reporting, immediate protection for “not mine,” case status and support handoff.
  • Accessibility, privacy-safe analytics, operational readiness and controlled pilot release.

1.2 Out of scope

  • Clinical accuracy, prescription validation or medicine-consumption inference.
  • Family assignment, refill reminder consent integration and reorder flows.
  • Penetration testing of any live environment without written authorization.
  • Validation of Medkart’s current internal retail software or production architecture.

1.3 Risk-based priorities

2.1 Test data principles

  • Use synthetic accounts, stores, invoices and item labels only.
  • Create explicit datasets for unique, missing, ambiguous and recycled-phone conditions.
  • Keep a deterministic event ID for replay and out-of-order lifecycle testing.
  • Do not copy customer phone numbers, medicine names, prescriptions or support notes into test evidence.
  • Version the approved savings comparator and record expected calculations independently.

4. Defect management

4.1 Severity model

SeverityDefinitionResponse
CriticalCross-account exposure, systemic authorization failure or severe privacy/security harm.Immediate no-go; restrict access; incident path.
HighCore data integrity, lifecycle, savings or correction failure with material user impact.Release blocker until fixed or formally risk-accepted.
MediumRecoverable functionality, accessibility or UX failure with a workaround.Resolve before pilot unless explicitly approved.
LowMinor presentation or clarity issue without material risk.Prioritize by value and capacity.

4.2 Required bug report anatomy

  • One specific summary: condition + behavior + impact.
  • Environment/build, test ID and reproducible steps.
  • Expected result, observed result and redacted evidence.
  • Severity based on impact, not emotion; owner and target milestone.
  • Privacy-safe data: no customer contact details, medicine names or clinical notes.
  • Retest result, regression scope and closure evidence.

Sample-defect disclosure: The workbook includes eight intentionally designed sample scenarios to demonstrate reporting quality. Their Evidence Type is “Sample scenario” and Status is “Draft.” They must not be presented as bugs found in Medkart.

5. Entry, exit and release decision

5.1 Test entry criteria

  • PRD acceptance criteria and MVP scope are stable enough to test.
  • Authorized environment, synthetic test data and required integrations are available.
  • Savings comparator/method and state mappings are approved for the build.
  • Logging is sufficient to diagnose without collecting unnecessary sensitive data.
  • Known limitations and owners are recorded.

5.2 Pilot exit criteria

  • All P0 cases pass; no P0 case is Not Run, Failed or Blocked.
  • No open Critical or High privacy/security defect.
  • No known duplicate counting, cross-account exposure or checkout-blocking behavior.
  • Savings explanation and unavailable states are approved and reconciled.
  • Support SOP, correction route, monitoring, feature flag and rollback are ready.
  • Accessibility and analytics checks pass for the pilot scope.
  • Go/no-go decision and risk acceptances are recorded by accountable owners.

5.3 Automatic no-go triggers

TriggerAction
Any cross-account exposure or authorization bypassDisable/restrict feature; security/privacy incident path.
Ambiguous identity reveals transaction metadataFail closed and stop rollout.
Savings is displayed without an approved comparatorSuppress module; stop rollout until corrected.
Linking failure interrupts checkoutDisable dependency; restore checkout independence.
Correction path cannot protect a “not mine” recordStop rollout and activate support fallback.

Decision rule: The workbook dashboard is a review signal, not an automated release authority. Product, Engineering, QA, Security/Privacy and Operations remain accountable for go/no-go.

6. Pilot, monitoring and rollback

6.1 Controlled pilot

  • Use a feature flag and small approved cohort of stores/accounts.
  • Confirm staff briefing, support route and escalation contacts before enabling.
  • Monitor match outcomes, duplicates, reconciliation delays, issue categories and invoice availability.
  • Review early cases daily; expand only after stable results and explicit approval.

6.2 Suggested operational indicators

IndicatorPurposeGuardrail
Eligible event → linked record rateDetect ingestion/match breakage.Segment safely; never expose unmatched detail.
Ambiguous/missing match rateMeasure identity friction.No automatic linking when uncertain.
Duplicate record rateDetect idempotency failure.Target zero; investigate immediately.
Reconciliation delayDetect queue or lifecycle lag.Alert on defined threshold.
”Not mine” issue rateDetect possible mis-linking.Privacy review and rapid protection.
Savings unavailable/error rateDetect comparator/data gaps.Suppress rather than estimate.
Support resolution timeCheck operational readiness.Do not promise an SLA until approved.

6.3 Rollback readiness

  • Named decision owner and technical operator.
  • Documented triggers, feature-disable steps and confirmation check.
  • Checkout remains unaffected when the app feature is disabled.
  • Queued events and linked records have an approved recovery/reconciliation plan.
  • Customer/support communication copy is ready for material incidents.

7.1 Recommended cadence

  • Daily during execution: failures, blockers, privacy risks and environment health.
  • Before pilot: evidence review against the 17-item release checklist.
  • During pilot: daily metric/issue review with a named decision log.
  • After pilot: findings, unresolved risks, customer/store feedback and scale recommendation.

Full detail per bug

BUG-001 — Ambiguous phone match briefly exposes a transaction card

  • Steps to reproduce: 1. Create ambiguous account match. 2. Process transaction. 3. Open candidate account before resolution.
  • Expected: No transaction metadata is visible until a unique authorized match exists.
  • Observed (sample): A card flashes with store/date/amount before protected state replaces it.
  • Suggested direction: Fail closed before rendering; add authorization check at API and UI cache boundary.
  • Owner: Unassigned · Target: Release blocker
  • Notes: Designed for interview exercise; not observed in production.

BUG-002 — Duplicate event creates two history records and double-counts savings

  • Steps to reproduce: Replay the same transaction event ID.
  • Expected: One idempotent record and one savings contribution.
  • Observed (sample): Two records appear and savings total doubles.
  • Suggested direction: Enforce unique event/transaction key and idempotent consumer.
  • Owner: Unassigned · Target: Release blocker
  • Notes: Hypothetical defect.

BUG-003 — Refund updates paid total but leaves original savings

  • Steps to reproduce: Link purchase, then publish full refund.
  • Expected: Returned state and savings reconciled to approved rule.
  • Observed (sample): Paid total is updated; savings remains in summary.
  • Suggested direction: Recompute materialized savings on lifecycle events.
  • Owner: Unassigned · Target: Release blocker
  • Notes: Hypothetical defect.

BUG-004 — Not-mine report leaves detail visible after submission

  • Steps to reproduce: Submit “This is not mine” and return to detail.
  • Expected: Detail is protected immediately.
  • Observed (sample): Line items remain visible until support closes case.
  • Suggested direction: Apply immediate protection state and purge local cache.
  • Owner: Unassigned · Target: Release blocker
  • Notes: Hypothetical defect.

BUG-005 — Invoice failure has no retry or support path

  • Steps to reproduce: Force invoice service error; tap View invoice.
  • Expected: Retry and support options appear.
  • Observed (sample): Generic error page with no recovery.
  • Suggested direction: Add recoverable empty/error state.
  • Owner: Unassigned · Target: Before pilot
  • Notes: Hypothetical defect.

BUG-006 — Issue status is conveyed only by color

  • Steps to reproduce: Use grayscale or screen reader on status screen.
  • Expected: Text/icon/accessible name communicates state.
  • Observed (sample): Only chip color changes.
  • Suggested direction: Add text status and semantic label.
  • Owner: Unassigned · Target: Before pilot
  • Notes: Hypothetical defect.

BUG-007 — Issue free-text note appears in analytics payload

  • Steps to reproduce: Submit issue note and inspect event payload.
  • Expected: No free text or medicine detail in analytics.
  • Observed (sample): Full note is included in generic analytics.
  • Suggested direction: Allowlist analytics fields; redact at client and collector.
  • Owner: Unassigned · Target: Release blocker
  • Notes: Hypothetical defect.

BUG-008 — Expired claim gives no safe support route

  • Steps to reproduce: Open an expired unmatched-purchase claim.
  • Expected: Clear reason and verified support route.
  • Observed (sample): Dead-end expired message.
  • Suggested direction: Add safe restart/support action without revealing record data.
  • Owner: Unassigned · Target: Before pilot
  • Notes: Hypothetical defect.
