Execution Workbook Guide

How to present and maintain this portfolio artifact honestly

ItemMeaningPortfolio use
RoadmapOutcome sequence with gates, owners and dependencies.Show that you can order work from discovery to pilot.
RICEReach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort.Explain assumptions; do not defend the score as objective truth.
MoSCoWMust, Should, Could and Won’t have for this MVP.Use explicit exclusions to protect focus.
Task trackerActionable work with owner, dependency and evidence.Demonstrates coordination and accountability.
Dependency registerConditions and risks outside a task owner’s direct control.Show early escalation and safe fallbacks.
Weekly statusConcise outcomes, next work, risks and decisions.Demonstrates stakeholder communication.
CompleteDeliverable exists and definition of done is met.Do not use for drafted or unverified work.
EvidenceAuthorized output, test result, decision or link.Never include customer PII or medicine details.
Suggested walkthroughDashboard → Roadmap → Prioritization → one task/dependency → Weekly Status.Keep to 3–4 minutes, then invite discussion.
DisclosureAll internal owners, dates, scores and states are proposed examples.Say this before presenting the workbook.
