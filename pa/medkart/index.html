Problem

Customers who buy from a Medkart store have no safe, durable record of that purchase inside the Medkart app — no visibility, no explainable savings, no reminders. Whether this gap actually matters to customers, and whether it's already solved internally, is unverified.

Role

Solo, independent candidate exercise — discovery interviews, a PRD, UX/feature spec, a clickable prototype, a QA plan and an operating playbook, done end to end the way the role would actually require.

Outcome / status

Draft for validation. Full document set and an interactive prototype are complete; nothing here is approved for implementation or claims access to Medkart's real systems.

This is the fully-developed piece of the portfolio: a complete, independent product exercise built specifically to show how the Product Associate role would be approached, from a two-store field visit through to a QA-ready release plan. It is not commissioned or approved by Medkart — every document says so, and every claim is labelled by how it was actually obtained.

ℹ

Evidence discipline — facts observed during two store visits are markedObserved; anything reconstructed from memory is Recalled; public app-store information is Public; everything else is an explicit, unvalidated Hypothesis. See the Discovery & Assumptions Brief for the full standard.
