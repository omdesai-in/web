What this means

  • Approved comparatorThe comparison must use a method reviewed by Medkart’s pricing and legal owners.
  • Same purchase contextOnly eligible items with a valid comparison are included.
  • Not always availableIf comparison inputs cannot be verified, the app should show the paid amount without a savings claim.
  • Method versionThis example uses “Comparison method v1” for transparency and audit.
